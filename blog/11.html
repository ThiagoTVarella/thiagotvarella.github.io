Neural networks are interpretable

→ Neural Networks Are Already Clean already-clean.goodfire.com

This is a very interesting post claiming that neurons in computational neural networks are way more interpretable than we thought. We see neurons being active to what looks like random concepts, and we call that polysemanticity. We thought that polysemanticity was caused by nonlinear, combinatorial, superposition of different unrelated concepts. In reality, the post claims a concept called recursive generalization can explain almost all neurons in a neural network. Recursive generalization means that a neuron can develop during training from simple concepts to represent things that seem unrelated. One example that it uses is 'iy' can develop to represent both 'Miyazaki' and 'do it yourself', or a neuron that started representing V-shaped figures can develop to represent tables (because of their corners) and cats (because of their snots).
