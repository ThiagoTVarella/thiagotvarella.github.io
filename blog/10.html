'In Fighting for Every Black Child, Did I Betray My Own?'

→ In Fighting for Every Black Child, Did I Betray My Own? nytimes.com

The story about a mother trying to balance providing a good education to her kid while improving the educational system in the US. Really well written and intimate, sharing many ups and downs of her journey as a parent while showing some realities of the racial and wealth disparity in education.
