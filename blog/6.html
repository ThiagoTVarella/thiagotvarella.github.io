A unified theory of Therapy based on Neuroscience

→ Therapy Needs Therapy nytimes.com

This essay describes how psychodynamics and CBT share a fundamental assumption, that is backed up by Neuroscience as predictive processing: the idea that your brain relies on your memory to interpret and re-shape reality.

Curiously, the author ends up basically proposing a new therapy modality, extrapolating conclusions outside of what the neuroscience fundamentals bring (the extrapolated conclusions were that safety and precision are vital to therapy). Nevertheless, a very interesting read!
