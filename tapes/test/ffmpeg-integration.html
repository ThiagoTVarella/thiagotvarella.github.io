ffmpeg.wasm integration check

The one part of the pipeline that cannot be tested with fakes: loading the wasm core, mounting a file through WORKERFS, detecting pauses, and cutting real MP3 chunks at them. Audio is generated here in the page, so nothing is needed but a browser.

First run downloads ~32 MB of wasm and is cached afterwards. Everything after that takes a second or two. Chrome or Edge on a desktop. Add ?core=<url> to load the wasm core from somewhere other than the CDN.
